Roni's MailMats

Open a mailbox and send each alt the things it can use, one click per alt. MailMats only drafts the mail: nothing leaves until you press Send yourself.

Getting started

Log in once on each of your characters. Every character remembers its professions and skill, its class and the item level of the gear it wears. WoW: Forever characters have a first name and a surname, and mail needs both, so MailMats stores the full name too.

Only characters on the same realm and faction are listed, since those are the ones you can mail.

The mailbox panel

Opening a mailbox docks a panel to the right of the mail window with your other characters. Each one gets a Send <profession> mats (N) button per crafting profession, where N is how many matching stacks you carry. "Mats" for a profession are every item that any of its recipes uses.

Clicking a button switches to the Send Mail tab, fills in the name and subject and attaches up to 12 stacks. Review the mail and press Send. If more stacks are left, click the button again after sending. Hover a button first to see exactly what would go.

Soulbound and locked items are never attached.

Keeping your own mats

Keep mats for my own professions (on by default) never drafts items the character you are playing uses itself. Choose picks which of your professions count. By default the crafting professions, Lockpicking and Comprehension count, while Cooking, First Aid and Poisons do not, so your Linen Cloth still reaches your tailor even though bandages use it too.

To keep one specific item out of every draft: /mailmats ignore [item].

Lockboxes, scrolls and crates

  • Rogue alts with Lockpicking get a Send lockboxes button (lockboxes, junkboxes, locked chests).
  • Mage alts with Comprehension get Send scrolls for the Forever mage scrolls.
  • Items above the alt's current skill are marked "(needs N)" in the tooltip; they are still sent.
  • Waylaid Supplies and Waylaid Crates go to the profession that fills them: cloth crates to Tailoring, ore and bars to Mining, herbs to Alchemy, and so on.

Gear upgrades and greens

Gear in your bags that an alt can wear right now and that has a higher item level than what the alt wears in that slot:

  • gets a small mail icon on its bag slot (default bags and Bagnon),
  • says "Upgrade for <alt> (+N item level)" in its tooltip,
  • is drafted by that alt's Send upgrades button.

"Can wear" checks level, the class's best armor type, weapon types the class can learn, and no off-hand while it wields a two-hander. Only item level is compared, not stats. Bind-on-pickup and soulbound items never count.

BoE green weapons and armor that are no one's upgrade go to your enchanter to be disenchanted (/mailmats greens off stops that). Greens mailed to another player ask cash on delivery, 100% of the vendor price by default; the Greens COD button on the panel changes the percentage.

Friends and guildies

Add a recipient... on the panel adds anyone: a name and a tick box per thing they want (any profession's mats, lockboxes, scrolls, your own groups). They get one Send all to <name> button. Right-click it to change what they get; the X removes them.

While you type, MailMats suggests friends, guild members, people you recently mailed and senders in your inbox. A mage gets scrolls ticked and a rogue lockboxes when you pick them.

Groups window

The minimap button (or /mailmats groups) opens every group with its items: each profession's mats, lockboxes, scrolls and your own groups.

  • Remove an item with its X; add one by dropping it on the window or typing its ID, link or name.
  • Edited built-in groups turn gold and can be put back with Reset to default.
  • New group makes a group of your own that you can give to recipients.

Mail messages

Every draft gets a short message in the mail body, picked from 100 in 20 tones (pirate, grumpy dwarf, secret agent, cozy grandma, corporate memo...), with the recipient's name and what is sent filled in. New message rolls another one. Anything you typed yourself is never replaced. /mailmats message off turns them off.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/mailmatsList your characters and their professions
/mailmats groupsOpen the groups window
/mailmats minimapHide or show the minimap button
/mailmats forget First LastForget a character you deleted
/mailmats ignore [item]Never draft this item (unignore undoes it, ignored lists them)
/mailmats target First Last: tailoring, scrollsAdd or change a recipient
/mailmats untarget First LastRemove a recipient
/mailmats targetsList recipients
/mailmats cod 50Cash on delivery for greens to other players, in % of vendor price (off to stop)
/mailmats greens offStop sending BoE greens to your enchanter (on again)
/mailmats message offNo messages in the mail body
/mailmats scanRe-read this character's professions

Good to know

An alt is missing from the panel

Log in on it once. Alts on another realm or faction are never listed.

An alt says "surname unknown"

It was saved by an older version. Log in on it once and the full name is stored.

Nothing was sent

That is on purpose: MailMats only fills in the mail. Check it and press Send.
