Opening it

Hold Alt and left-click an item almost anywhere: your bags and bank, the character sheet, loot, vendor windows, chat links, quest rewards, auction rows, and bag addons like Bagnon. Action bar buttons are skipped, because Alt-click there is self-cast.

/pedia opens and closes the window without an item.

The window

On the left: a search box (name or item ID) and two tabs, Bags with everything you carry and Recent with the items you looked at. On the right: the item, then its sections.

Every icon in the window is clickable. Click a reagent to see where it drops, click a recipe's product to see what it is used for, and so on. The arrows in the title bar, or your mouse's side buttons, go back and forward. Drag the title bar to move the window.

Recipes

ItemPedia knows every profession recipe in WoW: Forever, read from the game client's own data, so it does not matter which recipes you have opened or learned.

Created by

Every recipe that makes the item: profession, reagents and how many you get. Next to each recipe are its skill levels in the profession window's colours:

Skill 80 120 140 160

Orange: the skill you need to learn it. Yellow, green and grey: where skill-ups start to become less likely. Most trainer recipes have no orange number, because the game's files do not say when a trainer offers them.

Recipes your character already knows are marked (known).

Used in

Every recipe that uses the item, grouped by profession. Hover a recipe to see how many of the item it needs.

Teaches

Patterns, plans, formulas and other recipe items show the recipe they teach.

Where to learn a recipe

Under each recipe in Created by, Learn from says how you get it:

  • A recipe item (pattern, plan, formula...): its icon, which opens its own page, and where to get it: the nearest vendor that sells it, how many mobs or objects drop it, or the quest that rewards it.
  • A trainer: the three nearest trainers of that profession for your faction, with their title and distance. The nearest trainer may be too low a rank to teach a high-level recipe.
  • Automatically: some recipes come with the profession itself.

Where an item comes from

These sections use Questie's database:

  • Dropped by: mobs, with level, elite/rare/boss rank and zone.
  • Contained in: clams, lockboxes, satchels and other items you open.
  • Gathered from: herbs, ore veins and other objects.
  • Sold by: vendors.
  • Quest reward: quests that give it, with level, zone and quest giver.

Lists are sorted by drop chance when it is known, then by distance from where you stand, nearest first. Drop chances only exist for some items: Questie ships rates for quest-related drops.

Map button

Rows with a mob, vendor, object, trainer or quest giver have a map button on the right. Clicking it pins every spawn on your map (with Questie) and opens the world map on the nearest one. The zone and coordinates are also printed in chat.

Quests

Required for quests lists every quest that asks for the item, either as an objective (bring 10 Linen Cloth) or as something you must carry. Each row shows the quest level, the level needed to take it, the zone and the quest giver, with a map button to the giver.

  • Quests your faction cannot take are hidden.
  • Quests you already finished are greyed out, marked "(done)" and listed last.
  • Quests that start from an item have no map button.

Questie has no "which quests need this item" list, so ItemPedia builds one by reading every quest once, a few seconds after you log in. Until it finishes the section says "Scanning the quest database...".

Properties and tooltip

At the bottom: quality, type, slot, item level, required level, stack size, binding, vendor price, the Auctionator price when Auctionator is installed, how many you own (bags and bank) and the item ID. Below that, the item's full tooltip.

Good to know

Most sections are empty

Install Questie. Drops, vendors, objects, quests and map pins all come from its database.

The map button does nothing in combat

The game does not let addons open the world map during combat. Try again afterwards.

An item says "Retrieving item info..."

The game has not sent that item's details yet. It fills in after a moment.

Distances are missing

Distances only show for places on the continent you are on.
