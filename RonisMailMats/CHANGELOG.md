# Roni's MailMats

## v0.14.0

- **Recipes go to the profession that learns them.** Patterns, plans,
  schematics, formulas and recipes in your bags count toward that
  profession's Send button, so a Tailoring pattern goes to your tailor.
- **Spell reagents go to alts of that class.** Ankhs, Fish Oil and Shiny
  Fish Scales go to your shaman, Arcane Powder and runes to your mage,
  candles to your priest, and so on. The button only shows when you carry
  some. Reagents your own class uses stay in your bags.
- **Random subjects.** Each draft gets one of 12 subjects for what it
  holds: one list per profession and skill, plus upgrades, class reagents
  and mixed mails.
- **Mail messages are now off by default.** A mail with a message is not
  deleted when you take its items, so it fills the inbox until you delete
  it, and a full inbox queues new mail. Turn them on with the checkbox on
  the MailMats tab (it explains this) or `/mailmats message on`.

## v0.13.0

- **One minimap button for all Roni's addons.** Click it for a window with a
  tab per addon. Tabs of Roni's addons you don't have are greyed out;
  hovering one says whether it is not installed or turned off.
  It replaces MailMats' own button (its place and hidden state carry over).
  The MailMats tab opens item groups and the character list, and has the
  greens, message, cash on delivery and upgrade settings.
  `/mailmats options` opens it; `/mailmats minimap` still hides the button.

## v0.12.0

First release as its own addon. Until now MailMats was only available inside
the "Roni's One For All" bundle (v0.5.0).

- Opening a mailbox docks a panel listing your alts with a **Send
  <profession> mats** button each: it fills in the recipient and attaches the
  reagents that alt can use. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- Keeps mats for your current character's own professions.
- Lockboxes to rogue alts, scrolls to mage alts, and gear in your bags that
  is an upgrade for an alt (marked in your bags and tooltips).
- Friends and guildies as recipients, recipient groups, cash on delivery for
  greens sent to other players.
