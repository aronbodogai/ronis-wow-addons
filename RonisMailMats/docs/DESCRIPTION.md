Roni's MailMats
===============

Open a mailbox and a panel docks next to it with your alts. Each alt gets a
**Send Tailoring mats** (or Alchemy, Mining, ...) button that fills in the
name and attaches the reagents that alt can use. Nothing is sent until you
press Send yourself, so you always see the draft first.

**Made for WoW: Forever.**

**Full guide:** [aronbodogai.github.io/ronis-wow-addons/mailmats](https://aronbodogai.github.io/ronis-wow-addons/mailmats/)

- Mats your current character needs for its own professions stay in your
  bags.
- Lockboxes go to your rogue, scrolls to your mage.
- Gear in your bags that is an upgrade for an alt gets a little mail icon on
  its bag slot and a **Send upgrades** button.
- Friends and guildies can be recipients too, and so can groups of them.
  Greens sent to other players can ask cash on delivery.
- Keeps a history of everything you mail and receive, with a dashboard of
  what went to other players over the last day, week, month, half year,
  year and all time.
- Every draft gets a random subject for what it holds. Turn on random
  messages in the mail body too, from a pirate, a grumpy dwarf, a secret
  agent and more.

**One minimap button for all Roni's addons:** click it for a window with a
tab per addon you have installed. The MailMats tab has the groups,
character list, greens, message and cash on delivery settings.

Commands
--------

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/mailmats` | List your characters and their professions |
| `/mailmats groups` | Open the recipient groups window |
| `/mailmats selftest` | Check that the game gives MailMats what it needs |

The full command list is in the guide.
