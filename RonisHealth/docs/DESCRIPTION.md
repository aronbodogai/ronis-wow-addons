Roni's Health
=============

Small, honest reminders to look after yourself while you play.

**Made for WoW: Forever.**

- **Eye breaks (20-20-20):** every 20 minutes, look at something at least
  6 m (20 ft) away for 20 seconds and blink slowly a few times. Press
  **Start 20 s** and the card counts down for you.
- **Squat breaks:** every 40 minutes, stand up and do a short set. Choose a
  level that fits your body today: Gentle (5 chair stands), Standard
  (10 squats), Strong (15) or Athletic (20 slow squats). Calf raises and
  supported mini-squats are there for sore knees or tight spaces.
- Each reminder says in one line why it helps; **More** explains what the
  research actually shows, including its limits.
- Never in the middle of a fight: reminders wait until combat ends. The
  clock stops while you are AFK. Optional: no reminders in dungeons and raids.
- Snooze, skip, pause for an hour, and see how many breaks you took today.

Commands
--------

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/health` | Options window |
| `/health eye`, `/health squat` | Show that reminder now |
| `/health pause <minutes>` | No reminders for a while (default 60) |
| `/health resume` | End a pause |
| `/health status` | Time until each reminder |
| `/health stats` | Breaks taken today and in total |

General wellbeing suggestions, not medical advice.
