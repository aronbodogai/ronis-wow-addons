Roni's Health
=============

Small, honest reminders to look after yourself while you play.

**Made for WoW: Forever.**

**Works better with [OneForAll](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/oneforall-forever)**
(optional): Roni's Health reads its flight times to size squat breaks on
flights. Without it, flight times are estimated from the route's length.

- **Eye breaks (20-20-20):** every 20 minutes, look at something at least
  6 m (20 ft) away for 20 seconds and blink slowly a few times. Press
  **Start 20 s** and the card counts down for you.
- **Squat breaks:** every 40 minutes, stand up and do a short set. Choose a
  level that fits your body today: Gentle (5 chair stands), Standard
  (10 squats), Strong (15) or Athletic (20 slow squats). Calf raises and
  supported mini-squats are there for sore knees or tight spaces.
- Each reminder says in one line why it helps; **More** explains what the
  research actually shows, including its limits.
- Never in the middle of a fight: reminders wait until combat ends. The
  clock stops while you are AFK.
- No pop-ups inside dungeons and raids: when you come out, one card sums up
  how long you were in and which breaks you missed, with a button to take
  each one now.

  ![Back from Gnomeregan: missed 3 eye breaks and 2 squat breaks](https://aronbodogai.github.io/ronis-wow-addons/media/health-dungeon-summary.png)
- **Natural pauses:** take a flight, queue up a long batch of crafting, sit
  down, lie on your sleeping bag, sit at a campfire or sit down to eat, and a break that is nearly due (3/4 of
  its time) comes early. The card says why it showed up now.
- **Busy questing?** A break that comes due mid-pull waits for a calm
  moment, at most 5 minutes.
- **Squats that grow with you:** start at 8-10, and each week you keep up
  the range goes up a little (9-12, 10-13, ...). On a flight or a crafting
  queue the reps fit the time: a 3-minute flight gets two rounds, a
  1-minute one a single set.
- **Bedtime** (turn it on in the options): at 22:30, no new dungeons, time
  for easy stuff like a crafting queue while you brush your teeth. If you
  are in a dungeon it waits until you come out. From 23:00 a bedtime card
  every 10 minutes, with **Not tonight** to stop it.
- Snooze, skip, pause for an hour, and see how many breaks you took today.

Commands
--------

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/health` | Options window |
| `/health eye`, `/health squat` | Show that reminder now |
| `/health pause <minutes>` | No reminders for a while (default 60) |
| `/health resume` | End a pause |
| `/health status` | Time until each reminder |
| `/health stats` | Breaks taken today and in total |
| `/health bed on` / `off` | Bedtime reminders (`/health bed` shows one) |

General wellbeing suggestions, not medical advice.
