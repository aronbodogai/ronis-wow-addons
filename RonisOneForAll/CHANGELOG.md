# Roni's One For All

Every Roni's addon in one download. Each addon is its own folder and can be
turned off on its own in the AddOns list. Type `/roni` in game to see what is
installed and how to use it.

## v0.5.0

Roni's ItemPedia is now part of **Roni's One For All**. Updating installs
two new folders next to it: RonisOneForAll (the `/roni` command) and Roni's
MailMats.

**New: Roni's MailMats**

- Opening a mailbox docks a panel listing your alts with a **Send
  <profession> mats** button each: it fills in the recipient and attaches the
  reagents that alt can use. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- Keeps mats for your current character's own professions.
- Lockboxes to rogue alts, scrolls to mage alts, and gear in your bags that
  is an upgrade for an alt (marked in your bags and tooltips).
- Friends and guildies as recipients, recipient groups, cash on delivery for
  greens sent to other players.

**Roni's ItemPedia**

- Fixed: the v0.4.0 download also contained the MailMats folder inside the
  ItemPedia folder. It was never loaded; it is now its own proper folder.

## v0.4.0

Roni's ItemPedia:

- New **Required for quests** section: every quest that asks for the item,
  with quest level, required level, zone and quest giver, and a map button.
- Recipes show their skill levels and whether you already know them.
- **Learn from** under each recipe: the recipe item with its nearest vendor,
  drop or quest, or the nearest trainers of your faction.
- **Quest reward** rows show the quest giver with a map button.
